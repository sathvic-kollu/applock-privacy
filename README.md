# App Lock — legal pages

Privacy policy and terms for **App Lock** (`app.applock.fingerprint.password.vault`),
published on Google Play by Zoopet.

- Privacy policy: https://sathvic-kollu.github.io/applock-privacy/
- Terms: https://sathvic-kollu.github.io/applock-privacy/terms.html

The privacy policy URL is referenced in two places and both must match this one:
the Play Console listing, and `policy_url` in the app's `strings.xml`, which
`PolicyActivity` loads into a WebView from the first-launch consent screen.
